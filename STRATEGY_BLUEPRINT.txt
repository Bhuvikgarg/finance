1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CORE THESIS
================================================================================
Traditional indicators fail because they operate in isolation. 
The REGM-X Strategy generates edge by combining two institutional principles:

PRINCIPLE 1: "Markets Move on the Expectation Gap"
  Price does not move because news is good or bad; it moves when the reported
  reality or volume flow exceeds or contradicts what the consensus priced in.

PRINCIPLE 2: "Regime-Conditioned Volatility Barriers"
  Never use arbitrary fixed rupee/dollar stop losses. All targets and stops 
  must dynamically expand and contract based on 14-day Average True Range (ATR)
  and macro market regimes (Bull, Bear, Sideways).

2. THE 4 SPECIALIST AGENT CRITERIA (WHAT EACH AGENT MUST CONFIRM)
================================================================================

[AGENT 1: THE FUNDAMENTAL AGENT]
  - Debt-to-Equity < 1.5 (or healthy net-cash balance sheet).
  - Operating Margins are stable or expanding over the last 2 quarters.
  - Forward P/E is justified relative to historical peer averages.
  - VERDICT NEEDED: Business viability pass (Eliminate bankruptcy/fraud risk).

[AGENT 2: THE TECHNICAL & FLOW AGENT]
  - Macro Trend Alignment: Price > 50-day SMA.
  - Momentum: 14-day RSI between 45 and 68 (NOT in extreme overbought > 75).
  - Market Flow (Volume): Current volume > 1.2x of the 20-day moving average.
  - On-Balance Volume (OBV): Positive 10-day slope confirming accumulation.
  - VERDICT NEEDED: Liquidity and momentum confirmation.

[AGENT 3: THE NEWS & SENTIMENT NLP AGENT]
  - Real-time News Sentiment Index >= +15 on a -100 to +100 scale.
  - Absence of severe legal, regulatory (SEBI/SEC), or forensic fraud probes.
  - VERDICT NEEDED: Market psychology tailwind.

[AGENT 4: THE ANALYST & CONSENSUS AGENT]
  - Stock is trading below the Institutional Consensus Price Target.
  - Upside room to consensus target is at least >= +8%.
  - VERDICT NEEDED: Meaningful institutional runway.

4. TRIPLE-BARRIER EXIT & RISK MANAGEMENT RULES
================================================================================

Every single trade MUST have 3 active barriers placed immediately upon entry:

[BARRIER 1: THE PROFIT TARGET (UPPER BARRIER)]
  - Formula: Entry Price + (1.50 × ATR_14)
  - Action: Place a Limit Sell Order at this exact price.
  - Rule: When hit, exit 70% of the position to bank profit; move stop on 
    remaining 30% to breakeven (entry price) to capture runners.

[BARRIER 2: THE STOP-LOSS FLOOR (LOWER BARRIER)]
  - Formula: Entry Price - (1.00 × ATR_14)
  - Action: Place a Stop-Loss Market/Limit Order immediately upon entry fill.
  - Rule: Non-negotiable. If price touches this level, exit 100% of the position.
  - Risk-to-Reward Ratio: Pre-engineered at minimum 1.5 : 1.0.

[BARRIER 3: THE TIME HORIZON BARRIER (VERTICAL EXPIRATION)]
  - Formula: Entry Bar + 5 Trading Sessions (Max Holding Period).
  - Rule: If after 5 trading days neither the Profit Target nor the Stop Loss
    has been hit, liquidate the entire position at market close.
  - Reason: Dead money tying up capital in low-probability consolidation.

5. PRE-TRADE FAKEOUT & MANIPULATION CHECKLIST (DO NOT TRADE IF FLAGGED)
================================================================================

Before entering any position, run the following 4-step trap audit:

[ ] CHECK 1: VOLUME DIVERGENCE (BULL TRAP)
    - Did price make a 5-day gain while volume fell below 0.65x average?
    - If YES: REJECT TRADE. Smart money is not backing the breakout.

[ ] CHECK 2: MOMENTUM EXHAUSTION (RSI DIVERGENCE)
    - Did price hit a higher 10-day high while RSI made a lower high?
    - If YES: REJECT TRADE. Upward momentum is dying.

[ ] CHECK 3: "SELL-THE-NEWS" TRAP
    - Is news sentiment screaming bullish (+40/100) while daily price bar 
      closed red with heavy volume?
    - If YES: REJECT TRADE. Institutions are dumping into retail enthusiasm.

[ ] CHECK 4: OVERBOUGHT RUBBER-BAND
    - Is the stock more than +12% above its 50-day SMA with RSI > 78?
    - If YES: REJECT TRADE. Wait for a pullback to the 20-day SMA.

6. POSITION SIZING & CAPITAL ALLOCATION FORMULA
================================================================================

Never risk more than 1.0% to 1.5% of total account capital on a single trade.

FORMULA:
  Position Size (Shares) = (Total Account Capital × Max Risk %) / (1.00 × ATR_14)

PRACTICAL EXAMPLE:
  - Account Size: ₹500,000
  - Max Risk (1.0%): ₹5,000
  - Stock Current Price: ₹368.00
  - ATR_14 (1.0x ATR Stop Distance): ₹10.50
  
  Calculation:
    Position Size = ₹5,000 / ₹10.50 = 476 Shares
    Total Capital Allocated = 476 × ₹368.00 = ₹175,168
    Maximum Potential Loss if Stopped Out = 476 × ₹10.50 = ₹4,998 (Exact 1.0%)
